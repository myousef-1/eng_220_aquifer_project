## Outline

The slides follow the course grading rubric: slides 1 and 2 are the two-minute elevator pitch, and slides 3 to 21 are the seven minutes of content.

| Slide | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Project title and the full names of all team members |
| 2 | The project at a glance | What we're building, what it does, and who benefits |
| 3 | Background | Sandoval County's water resources and the concern about groundwater near Cuba |
| 4 | Research questions | What the project set out to answer |
| 5 | Deliverables | Each deliverable, who owns it, and when it's due |
| 6 | Data | The datasets used and where they come from |
| 7 | Methods | How the data were collected, cleaned, analyzed, and presented |
| 8–12 | Team contributions | One slide per member, presented by that member |
| 13–16 | Results | One chart per slide: groundwater levels, the Ojo Alamo and San Jose formations, monitoring coverage, and records near Cuba (from the [Analysis](analysis.md) page) |
| 17 | Findings | What the data show, what they suggest, and where they are too limited to tell |
| 18 | Limitations | Data gaps and what the results can't support |
| 19 | Risks | Project risks and how we reduce them |
| 20 | Timeline and milestones | The Gantt chart and course checkpoints, also on the [Timeline](timeline.md) page |
| 21 | Links | The datasets, GitHub repository, Streamlit app, and documentation |
