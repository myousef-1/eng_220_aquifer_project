# 6. Presentation and video

**Lead:** Petar Djordjevic

The team presents the project in class with a slide deck, and each member records their own video about their part. The slides are also part of the project's documentation: they summarize the work, show the results, and link to the data, code, and app.

## Slides

The slides are a working draft and will be updated as the team fills them in.

- [View the slides (PDF)](files/beneath_the_mesa_slides.pdf)
- [Download the slides (PowerPoint)](files/beneath_the_mesa_slides.pptx)

To update them, save the new version over `docs/files/beneath_the_mesa_slides.pptx`. Then export the PDF from PowerPoint with **File → Export** as `docs/files/beneath_the_mesa_slides.pdf`. Don't use Print to PDF, which drops the links on the last slide. Keep both file names the same so the links on this page keep working.

Every team member must submit their own copy of the .pptx on Canvas; group submissions don't count.

## Outline

The slides follow the course grading rubric: slides 1 and 2 are the two-minute elevator pitch, and slides 3 to 18 are the seven minutes of content.

| Slide | Title | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Project title and the full names of all team members |
| 2 | The project at a glance | What we're building, what it does, and who benefits |
| 3 | Background | Sandoval County's water resources and the concern about groundwater near Cuba |
| 4 | Research questions | What the project set out to answer |
| 5 | Deliverables | Each deliverable, who owns it, and when it's due |
| 6 | Data | The datasets used and where they come from |
| 7 | Methods | How the data were collected, cleaned, analyzed, and presented |
| 8–12 | Team contributions | One slide per member, presented by that member |
| 13 | Results | Charts, maps, and screenshots from the Streamlit app |
| 14 | Findings | What the data show, what they suggest, and where they are too limited to tell |
| 15 | Limitations | Data gaps and what the results can't support |
| 16 | Risks | Project risks and how we reduce them |
| 17 | Timeline and milestones | The Gantt chart and course checkpoints, also on the [Timeline](timeline.md) page |
| 18 | Links | The datasets, GitHub repository, Streamlit app, and documentation |
