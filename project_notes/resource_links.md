# Resource links

Sources Dylan Gonzales identified during data collection. The datasets the project used, and the ones it didn't collect, are listed on the docs site's [Data collection page](https://pdjordj11.github.io/eng_220_aquifer_project/data-collection/).

- USGS San Juan Basin digital aquifer data (ScienceBase): https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/5d8e350be4b0c4f70d0cc484
- USGS groundwater-flow model report, WRI 95-4187: https://pubs.usgs.gov/publication/wri954187
- New Mexico Bureau of Geology, San Juan Basin Brackish Water Assessment: https://geoinfo.nmt.edu/resources/water/projects/bwa/sjb/home.html
- New Mexico Office of the State Engineer, water and well resources: https://www.ose.nm.gov/
- National Ground-Water Monitoring Network (NGWMN) data portal: https://www.usgs.gov/tools/national-ground-water-monitoring-network-ngwmn-data-portal
- Healy Collaborative Groundwater Monitoring Network: https://geoinfo.nmt.edu/resources/water/cgmn/home.cfml
- New Mexico Water Data Catalog: https://catalog.newmexicowaterdata.org/
- New Mexico Water Data, mean annual depth to groundwater for the shallow riparian aquifer: https://catalog.newmexicowaterdata.org/dataset/mean-annual-depth-to-groundwater-for-the-shallow-riparian-aquifer
- New Mexico Interstate Stream Commission, acequias and community ditches: https://www.ose.nm.gov/Acequias/isc_acequias.php
